About Me

Background, economic philosophy, and research methodology.

Hello, I am Songkiat Panmart. As an economist, I am passionate about stripping away the convoluted jargon that often clouds economic discourse, adhering firmly to the guiding ethos: "Master Economics, the Simple Way."

My work focuses on synthesizing the principled rigor of classical price theory with the analytical power of modern game theory, environmental economics, and industrial organization. Whether evaluating macroeconomic policies, low-carbon industrial transitions, digital infrastructure rollouts, or strategic market design, I believe true economic clarity comes from tracking long-term secondary consequences and aligning underlying institutional incentives.

⚖ Unseen Consequences

Analyzing the indirect, systemic impacts of policy interventions across society rather than immediate short-term optical benefits.

♞ Strategic Interdependence

Formulating decision models that accurately reflect rational actor behavior, market structures, and equilibrium shifts.

◈ First-Principles Clarity

Translating complex econometric and game-theoretic models into transparent, actionable policy and business strategy.
