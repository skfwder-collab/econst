About Me

Background, economic philosophy, and research methodology.

Hello, I am Siwat Teimtad. As an economist, I am passionate about stripping away the convoluted jargon that often clouds economic discourse, adhering firmly to the guiding ethos: "Master Economics, the Simple Way."

My work focuses on synthesizing the principled rigor of classical price theory with the analytical power of modern game theory and behavioral economics. Whether evaluating macroeconomic policies, fiscal stimulus measures, or corporate competitive strategies, I believe the truest economic clarity comes from tracking long-term second-order consequences and aligning institutional incentives.

⚖ Unseen Consequences

Analyzing the indirect, systemic impacts of intervention rather than immediate political optics.

♞ Strategic Interdependence

Formulating decision models that accurately account for rational reactions and equilibrium shifts.

◈ First-Principles Clarity

Translating sophisticated mathematical models into clear, actionable economic insights.
